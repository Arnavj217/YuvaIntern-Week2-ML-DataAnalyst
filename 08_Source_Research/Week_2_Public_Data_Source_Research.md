# Week 2 Public Data Source Research

## 1. Government OGD — District-wise, season-wise crop production
URL: https://data.gov.in/resource/district-wise-season-wise-crop-production-statistics-1997
Publisher: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare / Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.
Use: crop area, production, yield and geographic/seasonal analysis.
The resource page states that the data are district-wise, crop-wise, season-wise and year-wise and was updated on 16 October 2024.

## 2. Government OGD — Rainfall in India
URL: https://data.gov.in/catalog/rainfall-india
Publisher: India Meteorological Department under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.
Use: monthly/seasonal rainfall and departure-from-normal variables for weather/yield analysis.
The catalogue describes month-wise all-India rainfall and sub-division-wise rainfall.

## 3. e-NAM
URL: https://enam.gov.in/
Publisher/implementation: National Agriculture Market under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, with SFAC as lead implementing agency.
Use: agricultural market information, price discovery, APMC/commodity analysis.

## 4. ES&E / Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
URL: https://desagri.gov.in/
Use: official crop production estimates and agricultural statistics for validation and reference.

## Simulation integrity statement
The CSVs in this package are synthetic examples intentionally containing missing values, duplicates, invalid values and outliers. They demonstrate the requested preprocessing workflow without presenting fabricated records as official data.
